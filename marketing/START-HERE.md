# Favourit marketing launch kit

Owner: Adam Levi · Public contact: adamzoharlevi@gmail.com

Start with 20 research interviews, then a supported cohort of up to 30 English-speaking adult creatives. Focus on visual design, short-form video and writing. Monthly cash ceiling: $300. Paid advertising: $0 for the first 90 days.

## Use the kit

1. Open the operating workbook in the delivered outputs folder. Change the start date if needed; dates are planning dates, not scheduled messages.
2. Use [Interview guide](INTERVIEWS.md) to learn about real needs. Record exact evidence in the Interviews sheet, without publicising participants' identities.
3. Use [Outreach pack](OUTREACH.md) for personalised invitations and one follow-up. You send messages yourself after selecting appropriate recipients.
4. Review [Content pack](CONTENT.md), replace marked placeholders, and post only after a public landing URL is available. No messages or posts have been sent by this kit.
5. Use [Programme operations](OPERATIONS.md) for screening, matching, onboarding, email consent and weekly reviews.
6. Follow [Release and measurement](RELEASE.md) before opening applications or recruiting people to provide real work.

## Current offer

Favourit helps independent creatives get the professional help they need by contributing the skills they already have.

> Your skills are your currency.
> Offer creative services, earn FAV, and use it to get design, video, and writing help from other creators.

Private pilot. FAV is usable inside Favourit. Cash-out is unavailable during this pilot. Joining does not guarantee orders, earnings, matches, cash value or future redemption.

The founder helps members scope their first service, onboard, find complementary opportunities, and provide feedback. The first cohort is limited by support capacity. It is not a scarcity countdown.

## Do not open service trading until

- 20 interviews are complete, and at least eight participants identify a concrete offer and need after understanding the restrictions.
- Actual external-user comprehension meets the four-of-five target.
- The order, fee, delivery, approval, dispute/refund and support flows have passed appropriate QA.
- Existing FAV allocations and service prices allow feasible exchanges. This launch kit does not issue FAV or change rewards.

Cash-out is a separate feasibility workstream. Document its funding, sale process, costs, limits and supported markets before making any new public promise.

## Approved language

Use: "a small supported pilot", "offer a clearly scoped service", "earn and spend FAV inside Favourit", "we are testing whether these exchanges are useful".

Avoid: "guaranteed clients", "earn cash", "risk-free", "free money", "hold for a better price", "the next Fiverr", fabricated user counts or invented results.

Review relevant customer feedback before changing the audience or offer. If most qualified people need cash, keep recruiting for research and resolve the payment proposition before asking them to work.
